Why does the cage curve?

C₆₀ is a hollow molecular allotrope of carbon. Its 60 atoms sit at the corners of 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons. The five-sided rings introduce curvature, closing the carbon network into a rounded cage.

12 pentagons+20 hexagons=32 faces

Topology check: 60 vertices − 90 edges + 32 faces = 2, Euler’s relation for a closed polyhedron.
